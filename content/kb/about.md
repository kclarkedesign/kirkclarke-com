**Where it started**
2007. Freelance web and design work for whoever would hire him — brand systems, WordPress sites, whatever a small business needed. No formal CS background, just enough curiosity to keep teaching himself the next layer down. Some of that teaching turned into an actual [beginner HTML course](/courses/beginner/html/getting-started.html) he still keeps running.

**Learning by doing**
Eight years at 92nd Street Y, watching the job get bigger than "designer" — a CMS migration, a Salesforce Marketing Cloud rollout, and in 2020, rebuilding how an entire organization delivered its programming online in a matter of weeks. Nobody hands you a playbook for that. You figure it out, or you don't ship.

**Where he is now**
Senior Director of Technology at The Writing Revolution, an education nonprofit, where he owns the integration layer connecting Salesforce, QuickBooks, and half a dozen systems that disagree about the same data — and leads the team that keeps it coherent. His boss and colleagues have called him a natural teacher, more than once. Outside of that: Koto and Kibi, two independent products, and a book he designed and edited for his mother.

He doesn't call himself the best at any one of design, engineering, or leadership. He calls himself resourceful — restless with monotony, most useful wherever the actual problem is, whichever discipline that turns out to require.

Off duty: dine-in movies at the Alamo Drafthouse (Resident Evil was the most recent), first- and third-person shooters — Destiny 2, Marvel Rivals, Arc Raiders.
