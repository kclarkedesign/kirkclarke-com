## The brief

A site for Daniella Rabbani — performer, singer, and podcast host — that could carry a concert announcement, an album release, and casting/booking inquiries at once, without feeling like three sites bolted together.

## What I built

Designed in Figma, with Claude Design in the loop for direction and prototyping, then built in Wix.

Design and build, start to finish.

- A dark, photo-led hero with a custom monogram in place of a wordmark.
- Editorial small-caps labels and generous whitespace, so a concert date and an album pre-save can sit on the same page as headshots without competing.
- One contact footer routing each kind of inquiry — bookings, management, commercial/voiceover, press — to the right place instead of a single generic form.
