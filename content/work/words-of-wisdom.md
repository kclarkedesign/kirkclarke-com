## The book

*Words of Wisdom: Jamaican Sayings, Their Meanings, and the Lessons of a Lifetime*, by K.L. Craigie — his mother. Fifty-five sayings she grew up hearing in Jamaica, spoken by the grandfather who raised her, by aunts, uncles, and neighbors. Each one is set down three ways: the original patois, its plain-English meaning, and a page to reflect on how it lives in your life today. Part proverb collection, part guided journal, part family heirloom.

## What I did

Design and editing. The real problem was structural: fifty-five entries, each carrying a quoted saying, a translation, and a full weekly journaling page, had to feel consistent across the whole book without ever feeling like a template stamped fifty-five times. Editing meant shaping her voice into that same repeatable structure without flattening it.

## Outcome

Published, available now — 52 weekly sayings plus three bonus ones, each with its own reflection page.
